# Northline Channel privacy policy (draft)

**Draft for legal review.** Fill in the bracketed items and have it reviewed before release.

- **No telemetry.** The plug-in does not collect or send usage data, analytics or audio.
- **Licence activation** (when licensing ships) sends the licence key, a hashed machine
  identifier and the product version to [licensing provider], only when you activate or
  deactivate. [Provider] processes this data as described in its privacy policy.
- **Crash reports** are off by default. If you opt in, a crash report contains the stack trace,
  OS version, host name and version, plug-in version and the plug-in state (parameter values).
  It never contains audio.
- **Presets and settings** are stored locally in your Documents and application-data folders.
- **Contact:** [privacy contact address]. Data requests are answered within 30 days.
