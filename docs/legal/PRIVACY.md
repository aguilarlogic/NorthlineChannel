# Privacy

Northline Channel does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- **No network access.** The plug-in never connects to the internet. It has no telemetry, analytics,
  licence activation, update check or crash reporting.
- **Local files only.** Presets and settings are stored on your computer, in your Documents and
  application-data folders (see the [manual](../manual/index.md)). They never leave it unless you
  copy them.
- **Audio** is processed in memory and is never written anywhere by the plug-in.
